How the CV pipeline works

① YOLO11n detects person class only (conf ≥ 0.35), ~10–16 FPS on the server.

② A centroid tracker keeps identities across frames (max 15 missed frames before expiry).

③ Occupant-based door logic: a person seen walking in from the waiting area and staying ≥ 2 s inside the doctor zone becomes an occupant and takes the next token; people already inside at start are ghosts. Exits are per person — a swap never completes the wrong token.

④ With the demo clip looping ping-pong (forward + reversed) the wrap is frame-continuous: the tracker is never reset. Jump-cut sources freeze events for 1.5 s and re-bind occupants instead.

⑤ With camera advances queue on (nurse desk), the next patient is admitted automatically, so the queue and the waiting count drop on their own.
